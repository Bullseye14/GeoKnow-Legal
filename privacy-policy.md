# Privacy Policy — GeoKnow

**Last updated: August 03, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how BullseyeGames ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with the mobile application GeoKnow ("the App") on Android and iOS.

GeoKnow does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you. The information described below is either stored locally on your device or collected in an anonymous form as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your game progress, experience points, statistics, unlocked content, language preference, and settings are stored **locally on your device**. This data remains under your control and can be erased at any time using the "Delete Data" option in the App's Settings screen.

---

## 2. Anonymous Analytics Data We Collect

To understand how the App is used and to improve it, we collect anonymous gameplay analytics at the end of each game session. This data is **not linked to your real identity** and includes:

- **An anonymous identifier** — a randomly generated ID (UUID) created on first launch. It is not connected to your name, email, phone number, or any account, and cannot be used to identify you personally.
- **Device information** — device model, operating system/platform, and App version.
- **Gameplay activity** — the game mode played, difficulty or sub-mode, session duration, your level, and the result of the session (such as score or whether a puzzle was completed).
- **Approximate location (country only)** — a two-letter country code derived from your IP address (for example, "ES"). We do **not** collect precise or GPS location.

This analytics data is transmitted securely and stored on our backend provider, Supabase. The country code is determined using the third-party service ipapi.co based on your IP address; we do not store your IP address.

---

## 3. Advertising

The App displays rewarded advertisements through **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect and process certain information, including your device's advertising identifier and interaction data, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. You can learn more at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and you can manage or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

---

## 4. Google Play Games Services

The App integrates **Google Play Games Services** to provide achievements and leaderboards. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, your gameplay achievements and leaderboard scores may be associated with your Google Play Games profile, as handled by Google. This is optional and managed by Google Play Games, not by us.

---

## 5. How We Use Information

- To operate, maintain, and improve the App and its game modes.
- To understand aggregate usage patterns through anonymous analytics.
- To display advertisements via Google AdMob.
- To provide achievements and leaderboards via Google Play Games Services.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent purposes. Anonymous data is processed by the service providers we rely on to run the App — namely Google (AdMob and Play Games Services), Supabase (analytics storage), and ipapi.co (country lookup) — solely to provide their respective functions.

---

## 7. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Anonymous analytics records are retained on our backend for as long as needed for analytical purposes.

---

## 8. How to Delete Your Data

You can erase all locally stored data (progress, statistics, and settings) at any time using the **"Delete Data"** option in the App's Settings screen. Because our analytics data is fully anonymous and not tied to your identity, it cannot be traced back to an individual user. If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, you may contact us at the email address below.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

GeoKnow is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information that identifies children. Because the App displays advertising and collects anonymous analytics, we encourage parents and guardians to supervise their children's use of the App. If you believe a child has provided us with information in a way that raises a concern, please contact us.

---

## 10. Data Security

All data transmitted from the App to our backend and service providers is encrypted in transit using secure (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
